The one thing we would still change
SEL writes the model as SEL-2414 on the product page and in the video title, but the approved caption file says SEL 2414, and so does our raw output, because that is how it is spoken. A single custom dictionary entry makes it SEL-2414 everywhere, for every future video, without touching the audio or the approved text. That is the difference between a transcript that is right and a transcript that is consistent with the rest of your library.
Custom dictionarysel-caption-dictionary.csv · 36 entries · 2 columns, from / to ↓ Custom glossarysel-caption-glossary.csv · 39 terms · 1 column, no header ↓

Both files are in the format Brightcove accepts on import: UTF-8, comma separated, no commas inside a term, one term per line. The dictionary is 2 columns with an optional header, the glossary is a single column with no header, and a term cannot appear in both.

What this page is showing

Captions, translation and accessibility, inside the video

Captions

Auto captions are part of the platform, not an AI Suite add-on. 94 base languages and 202 locale codes, videos from five seconds to four hours, roughly 1.5× the runtime to process, and bulk runs up to 100 videos at a time.

Line breaking is syntactic in 23 languages, so cues break on phrases rather than punctuation. The caption editor adds cue-level timing, a nine-position placement grid, find and replace, and a draft-to-publish gate per track.

Enhanced captions add speaker attribution and audio cues such as [music]. The track on this page uses them.

Dictionary: 1,000 entries, 2 columns, hard replacement. Glossary: 5,000 terms, 1 column, biases recognition.

Translation

Universal Translator covers 177 locale codes, about 153 languages. It translates full sentences with their surrounding context rather than cue by cue, which is why the Spanish reads like Spanish and not like subtitled English.

A translation glossary runs one per source-to-target pair, directional, with an exact locale match. Custom instructions are one account-wide prompt of up to 4,000 characters for register and do-not-translate rules.

Metadata language views carry the title and both descriptions into up to 40 languages. Tags stay in English, which matches how SEL already tags.

This page: five target locales, one glossary per pair, product and front-panel terms locked.

Voice

Standard dubbing speaks from your reviewed, glossary-controlled captions, so product names and protection terms stay exact. It is the cheaper path and the one on this page, in five languages.

Dubbing Pro writes its own speech-optimised translation fitted to the original timing and keeps the speaker's tone. It is the better fit for customer stories and executive messages, and it does not use glossaries.

Dubbed tracks sit in the player as audio tracks next to the original, and viewers can switch mid-playback.

Standard dubbing: 76 codes. Dubbing Pro: 103 codes with regional variants.

Accessibility

Captions cover WCAG 1.2.2. Audio description tracks or a polished transcript cover 1.2.3. Standard audio description fits AI narration into natural pauses and ships in 48 languages for 1.2.5, and extended audio description pauses the video and builds a described child video per language for 1.2.7.

The player here is Prism, the current build Brightcove describes as WCAG 2.2 AA, with a Deque VPAT from September 2026. Caption styling is left to the viewer, so nothing on this page overrides the browser's caption settings.

This supports your accessibility goals. It is not a compliance statement and not legal advice.

Player keyboard, screen reader and focus handling tested to WCAG 2.2 AA.
